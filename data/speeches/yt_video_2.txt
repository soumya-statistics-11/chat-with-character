Namaskar. Aaj hum sabhi deshvaasi ek mahaan aur mahatvapurn sankalp ke liye ekjut hue hain. Is sankalp ko lekar is karyakram se jude sabhi poojya sant jan, alag-alag rajyon ka pratinidhitva kar rahe sabhi rajyapal, sabhi mukhyamantri, anya sabhi janpratinidhi gan, 28,000 se adhik sthanon se jude ek crore se adhik mere yuva saathi — main aaj is shubh ghadi par shubh sankalp ke liye karodon-karodon naujawanon ka vishesh roop se abhinandan karta hoon.

Aaj hum sabhi jis abhiyan ke saakshi ban rahe hain, woh abhiyan vyakti ke liye hai, parivaar ke liye hai, samaaj ke liye bhi hai aur sabse badi baat, yeh rashtra ke liye hai.

Saathiyon, Viksit Bharat Sankalp Abhiyan ke liye Nasha Mukt Yuva — is movement ka naam hi iske sankalp ko spasht karta hai. Kyunki aaj jab desh ne Viksit Bharat ka lakshya tay kiya hai, to iske liye aavashyak hai ki desh ka yuva tan se swasth ho, man se swasth ho, aatmavishwas se bharpoor ho aur drugs jaisi ghaatak aadat se hamesha-hamesha door rahe.

Yahi is abhiyan ka uddeshya hai. Isliye hum sabko yeh sankalp lena hai. Hamare yuva nasha aur drugs ke khatron ko samjhein, har tarah se jagrook rahein aur usse door rahein. Aur is sankalp mein ab Rashtriya Nasha Mukt Yuva Abhiyan ki badi bhoomika hai.

Saathiyon, Kashi ka sansad hone ke naate mujhe is baat ki khushi hai ki is abhiyan ka pilot project ke taur par pichhle saal Kashi ki pavitra dharti se shuru hua tha. Isliye vaigyanik spashtata ke saath-saath ismein aadhyatmik urja bhi hai, santon ke aashirvaad bhi hain aur mahaan saanskritik viraasat bhi hai.

Ismein woh saanskritik samarpan bhi hai jisne sadiyon se nashe ko kharij kiya hai. Yahi wajah hai ki desh ke 125 crore se adhik aadhyatmik sangathan aur karodon-karod deshvasiyon ke hit ke liye aaj hamare saath jud chuke hain.

Saamaajik sangathanon ne, NGOs ne is disha mein sankalp liya hai. Sabne milkar Kashi Declaration ke zariye roadmap tay kiya hai. Aur aaj hum dekh rahe hain ki Kashi se utha woh kalyankari vichaar ab ek national project ban gaya hai.

Aaj se desh bhar mein Nasha Mukt Yuva Abhiyan aarambh ho raha hai.

Saathiyon, kuch kaam aise hote hain jo vyaktigat roop se koi prayas kare to kabhi-kabhi niraash ho jaata hai, thakaan mehsoos karta hai. Lambi doori chalne mein mann taiyaar nahi hota hai.

Lekin jab saamuhik abhiyan ban jaata hai, kandhe se kandha milaakar karodon log chal padte hain, to aise sabhi log jo vyaktigat roop se kuch apne aap nahi kar paate, unko bhi ek nai urja mil jaati hai.

Saamuhikta ki ek taakat hoti hai. Aur aaj us taakat ko lekar yeh abhiyan har yuva mann ko chhoone wala hai.

Thodi der pehle desh ke laakhon yuvaon ne nasha-mukti ki shapath li hai. Aapne apne jeevan ko nashe se door rakhne ka sankalp liya hai. Aapne apne school, apne college aur apne samaaj mein nasha-mukti ka sandesh pahunchane ka sankalp liya hai.

Saathiyon, aaj liya gaya yeh sankalp aane wale 100 weeks tak lagataar aise hi aage badhega.

Har Sunday kala, sanskriti, sports, meditation, adhyatm aur seva se judi activities hongi. Nasha-mukti ka sandesh naye logon tak pahunchega.

Aane wale 100 Sundays, Nasha Mukt Bharat ki disha mein 100 majboot kadam honge. 100 strong steps.

Saathiyon, aaj hazaaron schools, colleges aur universities ke laakhon students is programme se online jude hain. My Bharat ke volunteers bhi laakhon ki taadaad mein aaj isse jude hain. Mere NSS ke naujawan bhi aaj hamare saath shaamil hain.

Desh ke alag-alag kshetron se, alag-alag institutions se, alag-alag backgrounds ke 1 crore se adhik yuva aaj ek saath hain.

Mera aap sabse aagrah hai — aap sab is bade movement ke leader hain.

Aapne mere is vishwas ko aur majboot kiya hai ki Bharat ka yuva kitna jagrook hai, kitna samajhdaar hai aur apne kartavyon ke prati kitna gambhir hai.

Main aap sabko is abhiyan ke liye anek-anek shubhkaamnayein deta hoon.

Mere yuva saathiyon, aap Bharat ki Amrit Peedhi hain. Aap agle 20–25 varshon mein apne jeevan ko bhi disha denge aur aap hi 2047 mein desh ko Viksit Bharat ki manzil tak lekar jaayenge.

Aur uske shikhar par aap hi baithe honge. Uska saara rasapan karne ka avsar bhi aap hi ko milne wala hai.

Desh ko aapki energy, aapki imagination aur aapke talent ki zarurat hai. Isliye desh ke liye aur apne jeevan ke liye khud ko nasha-mukt rakhna bahut zaroori hai.

Nasha focus ko bhatkata hai aur dheere-dheere yuva ko uske apne sapnon se door le jaata hai.

Abhi jin naujawanon ne naatya manchan kiya, bahut kam samay mein bahut achhe tareeke se unhone samasya bhi batai, samasya ke kaaran paida hue sankat bhi bataaye aur baahar nikalne ke saamuhik prayatn ka raasta bhi dikhaya.

Naatya manch chhota tha, lekin jaisa unhone kaha, "Aao hum naatak dekhein." Yeh naatak nahi tha. Yeh hum logon ke liye ek bahut bada sandesh tha. Humein jaga raha tha, humein joojhne ke liye prerit kar raha tha.

Saathiyon, aajkal hum dekhte hain ki aaj ke yuva startups ki duniya mein chhaaye hue hain. AI ko lead kar rahe hain. Sports mein tiranga lehra rahe hain. Space se lekar science tak naye keertimaan bana rahe hain.

Lekin saathiyon, jab koi yuva drugs ke addiction mein phans jaaye, to keval ek yuva nahi haarta.

Ek sapna haarta hai. Ek sapne ki baal mrityu ho jaati hai. Ek parivaar haarta hai. Pura ka pura parivaar toot jaata hai.

Samaaj se bhi nafrat ka shikaar ban jaata hai.

Nasha keval shareer ko nuksaan nahi pahunchata. Nasha ek maa ki muskaan chheen leta hai. Nasha ek pita ke sapnon ko choor-choor kar deta hai. Ek behen ki rakhi ki ummeed ko kamzor kar deta hai. Ek chhote bhai ka aadarsh chheen leta hai.

Ghar ke buzurg har din is dard ko jeete hain ki unka apna bachcha dheere-dheere khud ko kho raha hai, khud ko gala raha hai, til-til gal raha hai.

Aankhon ke saamne use barbaad hote dekh rahe hain.

Aur jab parivaar mein, samaaj mein aisa hota hai, to kahin na kahin rashtra ki shakti bhi kamzor pad jaati hai.

Aur isliye dushman desh bhi shadyantra karke hamare desh ke yuvaon ko is nashe ki lat mein kheenchne ke liye koi koshish baaki nahi rakhte hain.

Drug supply karke kamaai karna aur hamare jaise desh ko tabaah karna — yeh bhi unki aatanki saazish ka hissa hota hai.

Aur isliye humein har ek yuva ko nashe ke changul mein phansne se bachana hai.

Saathiyon, nasha-mukt rehkar aapko apne potential ko protect karna hai.

Aapko dhyaan rakhna hai — drugs kuch samay ke liye high deta hai, lekin aapke career aur family life ko low kar deta hai.

Humein nashe ko kisi bhi tarah ki sweekaryata nahi deni hai.

Saathiyon, hamare samaaj mein bhi hamesha nashe ko ek burai ke roop mein dekha gaya hai. Hamare santon ne, gurujanon ne humein isse bachne ke liye chetaaya hai.

Shri Guru Granth Sahib mein bhi kaha gaya hai:

"Jitu peetai mati doori hoi, baralu pavai vich aai; aapna paraia na pachhano, khasamahu dhake khai."

Arthaat, jis padaarth ke sevan se buddhi aur vivek saath chhod dein, insaan apne aur paraaye ki pehchaan bhool jaaye — aisa nasha use patan ki or le jaata hai.

Saathiyon, hamari sanskriti mein bachaav ke liye chetaavni bhi di gayi hai aur nashe ki aadat lagne par usse nikalne ke raaste bhi bataaye gaye hain.

Samaaj ka sahyog, yog aur dhyaan ki taakat — yeh nashe ke khilaaf hamare antarman ko taakat dete hain.

Aur aaj to hamare paas nasha chhodne ke liye rehabilitation centres jaisi suvidhaayein bhi hain.

Isliye agar koi yuva galti se nashe mein phans bhi gaya hai, to iska yeh matlab bilkul nahi hai ki uske saare raaste band ho gaye hain.

Humein hamesha is baat ka dhyaan rakhna hai.

Ghar ka koi bachcha agar nashe ka shikaar ban gaya hai, to ise saamajik pratishtha ki aad mein chhupaayein nahi.

Jiski zarurat ho, uska sahyog maangein. Mann kholkar usse baat karein. Jo experts hain, unka sahyog lein. Medical help se apne bachche ko nashe se mukt karaane ka prayaas karein.

Humein parivaar mein bachchon se khulkar samvaad ki culture bhi banaani chahiye, taaki aise bhanwar mein phansne se pehle hi aap uska haath thaam sakein.

Aapko bhanak aa jaayegi. Thoda dhyaan rakhiye, pata chalega ki usmein badlaav aa raha hai.

Woh khoya-khoya lag raha hai. Woh munh chhupa raha hai. Kamre mein band ho ja raha hai. Der raat aa raha hai. Paise anap-shanap maang raha hai.

Yeh sab cheezon se pata chal jaata hai, bhanak aa jaati hai ki kuch maamla gadbad hai.

Saathiyon, ek aahvaan main college aur universities ke hamare professor saathiyon se bhi karna chahta hoon.

Aap bhi course ki padhaai ke saath-saath students ke saath drugs ke khatron par baat karein. Nashe ke khilaaf apne campus mein ek movement chalaayein.

Aapke prayaas bade-bade parinaam la sakte hain.

Aur saathiyon, humein ek aur baat dhyaan rakhni hai.

Jo yuva nashe se ladkar baahar nikalte hain, ve kamzor nahi hote. Jo nashe ko paraast karke nikla hai na, woh asli yoddha hota hai.

Samaaj ko unka samman karna chahiye. Apnapan dena chahiye. Use doosra mauka dena chahiye.

Kyunki drugs se juda kisi ka ateet uski pehchaan nahi ho sakta.

Uska saahas uski pehchaan hota hai.

Saathiyon, drugs ke khilaaf abhiyan mein aaj sarkaar bhi majbooti se yuvaon ke saath khadi hai.

Desh mein nashe ke kaarobariyon aur taskaron par sakhti ke saath kaarvaai ho rahi hai. Pehle ki tulna mein kai guna zyada giraftariyaan hui hain. Hazaaron crore rupaye ki drugs bhi jabt ki gayi hain.

Yeh dikhaata hai ki drugs ke khilaaf hamari sarkaar kitni sakht hai.

Mujhe vishwas hai ki Kendra Sarkaar, Rajya Sarkaar — har koi ise mission mode mein karega.

Mujhe vishwas hai ki hamare desh ka yuva nasha-mukt rehkar apni yuva energy ko na keval sanrakshit karega, balki Viksit Bharat ke sankalpon ko bhi aage badhaayega.

Aur jab main aaj anek mahaan santon ko mere saamne dekh raha hoon, door se main mann se unko pranaam karta hoon.

Main in sabhi samaajik sansthaon, aadhyatmik sansthaon aur aadhyatmik gurujanon ka hriday se aabhaar vyakt karta hoon.

Aapka desh ke kone-kone mein sangathan hai. Aapke yuva saathi devotees hain.

Aane wale 100 din mein har ek sangathan ek hafta apne zimmedari le sakta hai. Pura hafta poore desh mein woh sangathan leadership kare. Baaki sabhi shaktiyaan uske saath judhein aur Sunday ko bahut bada programme karein.

Doosre hafte doosra sangathan, doosri shakti hafta bhar prayaas kare, programme kare aur Sunday ko bada programme kare.

Agar 100 weeks hamare desh ke aise aadhyatmik, samaajik aur yuva sangathan ek-ek hafta apne zimmedari le lein aur baaki saari shaktiyaan jud jaayein aur Sunday ko sab milkar karein, to aap kalpana kar sakte hain ki desh mein kitna bada aandolan khada ho jaayega.

Main ek baar phir aap sabne samay nikala, is pavitra kaarya mein hum sabko aashirvaad diye — iske liye santon ka, aachaaryon ka, sangathan ke mahaarathiyon ka hriday se aabhaar vyakt karta hoon.

Main in ek crore se zyada jude naujawanon ka vishesh roop se abhinandan karta hoon.

Aapne bahut bada kaam kiya hai.

Main My Bharat ke volunteers ko badhaai deta hoon. Woh jo bhi thaan lete hain, aajkal karke dikhaate hain.

Abhi pichhle dino mujhe My Bharat ke volunteers se milne ka mauka mila. Ve Hindustan ke seemavarti gaonon mein jaakar ek-ek hafta rukkar aaye hain.

Jise kabhi desh ka aakhiri gaon kaha jaata tha, jise aaj hum desh ka pehla gaon kehte hain, wahan jaakar aaye.

Unke anubhav bade prernaadayak the.

Aur aaj itna bada programme My Bharat ke volunteers kar paate hain, yeh apne aap mein unki rashtra ke prati jaagruk aur samarpit bhaavna ka prateek hai.

Main unki bhi badhaai deta hoon, unka bhi abhinandan karta hoon.

Aur mujhe poora vishwas hai ki aap sabke saamuhik prayaason se yeh movement ghar-ghar pahunchegi, har yuva ke dil mein pahunchegi aur yeh drugs ke kaarobariyon ke liye bahut bada khauf bhi banegi.

Aaiye, hum sab milkar aage chalein.

Meri aap sabko bahut-bahut shubhkaamnayein.
