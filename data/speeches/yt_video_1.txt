Sathiyo, Kovind ji ne apne jeevan me Morarji Bhai Desai ke saath anubhavon ke baare me bhi isme vistaar se bataya hai. Kovind ji ke bheetar desh seva ki jo bhavna thi, Morarji Bhai ke saath kaam karke unhe jo seokhne ko mila, jo rasta mila, Kovind ji ne rajneeti aur samaj seva ko apna maarg banaya. [Gala saaf karne ki aawaaz] Wo apne kartavyon ke liye samarpit rahe. Seva ko unhone apna lakshya banakar kaam kiya. Sansad me unka kaaryakaal gawah hai. Rajyapal ke roop me bhi unhone ek udaharan prastut kiya. Rashtrapati ke roop me bhi [Gala saaf karne ki aawaaz] hamein unki yahi kartavya nishtha dikhai deti rahi. Yahan tak ki Rashtrapati jaise sarvochha pad se seva mukt hone ke baad bhi unhone vishram nahi liya hai. Wo abhi bhi Ek Desh Ek Chunav, One Nation One Election jaise mahatvapurna vishay par kaam kar rahe hain. Desh ko jaga rahe hain. [Prashansa] Ye ek aisa vishay hai jiska samadhan desh ke loktantra ka naya adhyay shuru kar sakta hai. Main maanta hoon Kovind ji ki is kartavya nishtha aur seva bhaav se desh ke log bohot kuch seekh sakte hain.
