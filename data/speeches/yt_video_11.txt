Bharat Mata ki Jai!

Aaj Punjab di is Guru-Peera di dharti te, Dera Sachkhand de vich aa ke mainu maan mehsoos hoya hai. Punjabo, ki haal hai? Chadhdi Kala!

Saathiyon, kuch samay pehle main yahan Shri Guru Ravidas Ji ke janmotsav ka hissa banne aaya tha aur aaj hamara Jalandhar vikas ke utsav ka saakshi ban raha hai.

Aaj subah mujhe Jind se desh ki pehli hydrogen train shuru karne ka avsar mila hai. Aaj Jind se hi mujhe Kurukshetra mein ek bhavya Sikh Museum ki aadhaarshila rakhne ka saubhagya mila hai.

Yeh museum hamari Guru parampara ko, Gurujanon ke gyaan ko bhaavi peedhi tak pahunchane mein badi bhoomika nibhaayega. Yeh Gurujanon ki shikshaon, unke balidaan aur seva ki parampara ko jeevant roop mein prastut karne wala prerna kendra banega.

Haryana ki BJP government ne hamari Sikh viraasat ko bhaavi peedhi tak pahunchane ke liye yeh pavitra kaam shuru kiya hai.

Saathiyon, vikas karyon ko gati dete hue ab yahan Jalandhar se kai nai rail services shuru ki gayi hain.

Aaj Amritsar se Kashi ke beech rail service bhi shuru hui hai. Isse Guru Ravidas Ji ke bhakton ke liye Punjab se Kashi jaana aur adhik aasaan ho jaayega.

Aaj Daulatpur Chowk aur Kartoli ke beech nai railway line bhi shuru hui hai. Kartoli se Ambala ke beech nai rail service ko bhi hari jhandi dikhaayi gayi hai.

Saathiyon, aaj hi Ludhiana bypass ki aadhaarshila rakhi gayi hai aur Delhi-Amritsar-Katra Expressway ke ek aur section ka lokarpan hua hai.

Main aise har project ke liye deshvaasiyon ko aur khaastaur par Punjab ke mere bhai-behnon ko bahut-bahut badhaai deta hoon.

Aaj Bharat Sarkaar connectivity aur infrastructure ke anek aise projects par Punjab mein kaam kar rahi hai.

Punjab mein BJP ki government nahi hai. Lekin hamara Punjab viksit ho, iske liye Kendra ki BJP-NDA government koi kasar baaki nahi chhod rahi hai.

Saathiyon, aaj main vishesh roop se Jalandhar ke logon ki bhi sarahana karunga, jinhone "Swachhta Se Swagat" pahal ke maadhyam se is programme ko aur saarthak bana diya hai.

Shri Guru Ravidas Ji ne samrasta aur saamuhik prayaason ka jo sandesh diya tha, use aap sabhi ne apne vyavahaar mein jeevant kiya hai.

Yeh abhiyan kartavya-bhaavna ka bhi uttam example ban raha hai.

Saathiyon, aaj ke din main is manch se Punjab ke mahaan sapoot Sardar Teja Singh Samundari Ji ko bhi apni shraddhanjali deta hoon.

Aaj unka 100vaan shahadat diwas hai.

Unhone British shaasan kaal mein Sikh hiton ki raksha ke liye anek aandolan kiye. Sardar Teja Singh Ji ne apna poora jeevan matribhoomi ki seva mein samarpit kar diya.

Aaj se shuru ho rahe unke shahadat varsh se jude programmes se hum sabko prerna milegi.

Saathiyon, Kendra ki BJP government ki bhi priority hai — Bharat ke har nagrik ki seva, Bharat ke har nagrik ka jeevan aasaan banaana aur use aage badhne ke liye naye-naye opportunities available karaana.

Aur isliye hum **"Nagrik Devo Bhava"** ke mantra par chal rahe hain.

Gareeb ho, middle class ho, aise har parivaar ko best se best facilities milein — yeh hamara prayaas hai.

Aur Nagrik Devo Bhava ka yeh bhaav hamare kaam mein dikhta hai.

Saathiyon, jo Indian Railways hai, woh gareeb parivaaron aur middle-class parivaaron ki sawaari hai.

Lekin 2014 se pehle tak railway ki kya situation thi, yeh aap achhe se jaante hain.

Congress government nai trains ki jhoothi announcements karke aaraam se baith jaati thi.

Hamare jo railway stations hain, inko to Congress ne apne haal par chhod diya tha.

Yahan tak ki Angrezon ke zamaane mein jo stations bane the, unki sudh lene wala bhi koi nahi tha.

Saathiyon, Indian Railways ka network itna vishaal hai ki roz usmein jitne passengers travel karte hain, utni to kai deshon ki population bhi nahi hai.

Railway development ke jo kaam aaj ho rahe hain, yeh 30-40 saal pehle ho jaane chahiye the.

Lekin pehli ki governments ki priorities mein yeh kaam tha hi nahi.

Gareeb aur middle class ko bhi suvidha chahiye. Inko bhi safai chahiye. Inke samay ki bhi value hai.

Yeh pehle ki governments charcha tak nahi karti thi.

Saathiyon, aisi soch se Viksit Bharat nahi ban sakta tha.

Isliye beete varshon mein BJP government ne situations ko badalna shuru kiya.

Aur inmein se kai stations par kaam poora bhi ho chuka hai.

Aaj aise hi 75 stations ka lokarpan kiya gaya hai.

Saathiyon, jab aap yahan Jalandhar Cantt station mein jaayenge, tab aapko ehsaas hoga ki waaqai hamara desh developed nation banne ki or badh raha hai.

Yeh keval station nahi hai. Yeh ek prakaar se city centre hai.

Jalandhar Cantt station modern bhi hua hai aur iski capacity bhi bahut badh gayi hai.

Aise hi Anandpur Sahib, Muktsar aur Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar stations ka bhi makeover ho gaya hai.

Saathiyon, hamare naye railway stations trade aur business ka bhi madhyam ban rahe hain.

Yahan jo local artisans hain, Vishwakarma saathi hain, mahilaon ke self-help groups hain, unke products station par bik sakein — aisi vyavastha banaayi gayi hai.

Aur yeh Amrit Bharat stations hamari viraasat, hamari dharohar ke darshan bhi karaate hain.

Ab jaise Jalandhar hai, yahan ki pehchaan sports se hai, khel-kood se hai, to Jalandhar Cantt station mein Punjab ki sports tradition aur lok-sanskriti ke darshan hote hain.

Aap Anandpur Sahib station jaayenge, to wahan Takht Shri Kesgarh Sahib ki jhalak dikhaai deti hai.

Kalka station par Shri Kali Mandir ke darshan honge.

Yaani hamare stations ab hamari culture aur civilisation ke symbols bhi ban gaye hain.

Saathiyon, hum apne purvajon se prerna lete hue, unka maan badhaate hue hi ek sashakt rashtra ban sakte hain.

Isi pavitra bhaav se hi Adampur Airport ka naam **Shri Guru Ravidas Ji Maharaj Airport** kiya gaya hai.

Aur Chandigarh International Airport ko ab **Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport** ke naam se jaana jaata hai.
