Sathiyo, viksit gaon, viksit Bharat ki aadharshila hain. Viksit Bharat ke nirmaan ki yatra lagataar chal rahi hai aur isme aap sabhi yuva sathiyo ki badi bhoomika hai. Sathiyo, is abhiyaan me aapne anubhav kiya hoga ki border ke gaonon ka jeevan kitna kathin hota hai. Lekin har kathinai ke baavajood, seemavarti gaonon ke log deshbhakti ko pal-pratipal jeete hain. Kuch varsh pehle tak toh border ke gaonon ko 'antim gaon' kahkar apne haal par chhod diya gaya tha. In gaonon me sadak, bijli, paani, school, haspatal—aisi har basic suvidhaon ka abhaav tha. Sambhav hai ki aapne bhi aisi anek kahaniyan wahan ke logon se mehsus ki hongi, pehle ke haal unhone bataye honge. Isliye sathiyo, hamari sarkar ne in seemavarti gaonon ko desh ka 'pratham gaon' maana. Woh pratham gaon jahan subah ki pehli kiran Bharat ka abhinandan karti hai aur jahan aakhri kiran 'Good Night' kah ke vida leti hai. Shaharon me yeh samanya suvidhaayein lagti hain, lekin wahan ke logon ke liye yeh jeevan badalne wali suvidhaayein hain.

Iske kaaran wahan kisaan-bagwaan ka jeevan badal raha hai. Phal-sabzi badi-badi mandiyon tak pahunchana aasaan hua hai. Behtar connectivity ke kaaran ab jeevan rukta nahi hai, non-stop chalta hai. Aur aapne yeh bhi dekha hoga ki aaj door-sudeur ke in gaonon me bhi behtareen internet connectivity hai. Aap jo sathiyo ne bataya, kitni khushi se bata rahe the.
