Your Excellency Prime Minister Meloni, dono deshon ke delegates, media ke saathiyon — Namaskar!

Bonjourno!

Mere aur mere delegation ke bhavya swagat ke liye main Prime Minister Meloni ka hriday se aabhaar vyakt karta hoon.

Rome ko vishwa mein **Eternal City** ke roop mein jaana jaata hai. Bharat mein meri Lok Sabha constituency Kashi ko bhi anaadi aur shaashwat nagar maana jaata hai.

Jab do sabhyataayein milti hain, to baatcheet keval agenda tak seemit nahi hoti. Ismein itihaas ki gehraai, bhavishya ki drishti aur mitrata ki sahajta — teeno saath dikhai dete hain.

Friends, pichhle lagbhag 3 varshon mein mujhe kai baar Prime Minister Meloni se milne ka avsar mila hai. Yeh Bharat aur Italy ke beech kareebi sahyog aur saamanyas ko darshaata hai.

Unke leadership mein hamare relations ko nai gati, nai direction aur naya confidence mila hai.

Mujhe khushi hai ki hum apne relations ko upgrade karte hue **Special Strategic Partnership** ki ghoshna kar rahe hain.

Friends, aaj ki meeting mein humne hamari future partnership ko aur sashakt banaane ke liye vistaar se charcha ki.

**India-Italy Joint Strategic Action Plan 2025 to 2029** hamari partnership ko ek practical aur futuristic framework provide karta hai.

Hum is par time-bound tareeke se aage badh rahe hain.

Hamare shared efforts mein bilateral trade ke **20 billion euro** ke target ki or hum tezi se badh rahe hain.

Bharat mein maujood **800 se adhik Italian companies** Bharat ki growth story mein active partners ban rahi hain.

Aaj aayojit kiya ja raha Business Forum yeh spasht karta hai ki dono deshon ke business leaders mein naya enthusiasm, naya confidence aur naya ambition hai.

Italy duniya mein design aur precision ke liye jaana jaata hai.

Bharat ki pehchaan scale, talent aur affordable innovation ke powerhouse ki hai.

Isliye hum **"Design and Develop in India and Italy, and Deliver for the World"** ke principle par aage badhenge.

Fashion se lekar fintech tak, leather se lekar logistics tak, mobility se lekar manufacturing tak — har field mein hum apni strengths ko jodkar poori humanity ki bhalai ke liye kaam karenge.

**India-EU FTA** ke conclusion mein Italy se mile support ke liye main Prime Minister Meloni ka vishesh roop se aabhaar vyakt karta hoon.

Hum milkar iske jald se jald implementation ke liye kaam karenge, jisse business communities iska poora laabh utha saken.

Friends, technology aur innovation hamari partnership ka engine hai.

AI, quantum, space aur civil nuclear energy jaise fields mein hamare cooperation ki apaar possibilities hain.

Dono deshon ke startups, research centres aur industries ko aapas mein jodne ke liye hum **India-Italy Innovation Centre** par kaam kar rahe hain.

Friends, defence aur security ke fields mein kareebi cooperation hamare gehre mutual trust ka prateek hai.

Hamari armies ke saath-saath dono deshon ke defence industries ke beech bhi cooperation badh raha hai.

Hamare **Defence Industrial Roadmap** se co-development aur co-production ka maarg prashast hua hai.

Maritime powers ke roop mein Bharat aur Italy ke beech connectivity ke field mein kareebi cooperation swabhavik hai.

Hum milkar shipping, ports, modernisation, logistics aur blue economy par kaam karenge.

Humne apni win-win partnership ko third countries tak le jaane par bhi charcha ki.

Is direction mein aage badhte hue hum Africa mein concrete projects par kaam karne ke liye sahmat hue hain.

Friends, Bharat aur Italy ekmat hain ki terrorism humanity ke liye serious challenge hai.

Terror financing ke khilaaf hamari shared initiative ne poori duniya ke saamne ek important example present kiya hai.

Bharat aur Italy ne yeh clear message diya hai ki **responsible democracies** keval terrorism ki ninda nahi karti, balki uske financial networks ko todne ke liye bhi concrete steps leti hain.

Ukraine, West Asia aur anya tensions ko lekar hum lagataar contact mein rahe hain.

Bharat ka mat spasht hai ki sabhi problems ka solution **dialogue aur diplomacy** ke maadhyam se hona chahiye.

Friends, hamare historical, cultural aur people-to-people relations ki jhalak dono deshon mein kadam-kadam par dikhai deti hai.

Italy mein Indian art, yoga, Ayurveda aur khaana-paan ke prati attraction badh raha hai.

Sanskrit aur Latin jaisi ancient languages ek hi language family se aati hain.

Yeh darshaata hai ki hamara relationship keval diplomacy ka nahi, balki **shared heritage** ka hai.

Agle varsh Bharat aur Italy ke relations ki **80th anniversary** ke upalakshya mein **Year of Culture** manaaya jaayega.

Isse hamare cultural relations ko aur strength milegi.

Italy mein badi sankhya mein reh rahe Indian-origin log hamare relations ki living bridge hain.

Unki dekhbhaal ke liye Prime Minister Meloni aur Italy ke logon ka main hriday se dhanyavaad karta hoon.

3 saal pehle kiye gaye **Migration and Mobility Partnership Agreement** se dono deshon ke logon ke beech movement badha hai.

Hum yeh win-win cooperation jaari rakhenge.

Excellency, aaj ki bahut hi useful meeting ke liye aur aapki friendship ke liye main ek baar phir aapka hriday se aabhaar vyakt karta hoon.

140 crore Bharatvaasiyon ki or se main aapko Bharat aane ka ek baar phir se nimantran deta hoon.

Bahut-bahut dhanyavaad.

**Grazie mille!**
