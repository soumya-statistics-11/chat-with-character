Elle ge nanna namaskara!

Gorakhpur Ramakrishna Math aur Ramakrishna Mission Belur Math ke adhyaksh Param Poojya Swami Gautamanandaji Maharaj, Shri Ramakrishna Ashram Mysore ke adhyaksh Poojya Swami Muktidanandaji, Ramakrishna Math aur Ramakrishna Mission ke trustee Poojya Swami Yadvendrajii, Rajyapal Shri Thawar Chand Gehlot ji, Mukhya Mantri Shri D. K. Shivakumar ji, Sansad Yaduveer Krishnadatta Chamaraja Wadiyar ji, Vidhayak Vijayendra Yediyurappa ji, K. Harish Gowda ji, anya mahanubhav, poojya santgan aur bhaiyon aur behnon!

Sarvapratham main Mysore ki adhishtatri devi Maa Chamundeshwari ko pranam karta hoon. Abhi do din pehle hi hum sabhi ne Guru Purnima bhi manai hai. Aap sab jaante hain meri yatra jahan se shuru hui aur aaj main jahan hoon, isme Ramakrishna Mission ka bahut aham yogdan hai. Main teen mahine pehle Howrah me Belur Math gaya tha. Sabhi poojya santon se satsang karne ka avsar mila tha. Mera saubhagya hai ki aaj mujhe phir Mysore me Shri Ramakrishna Ashram se jude santon se, mere guru-bandhuon se milne ka, unse aashirvaad lene ka avsar mila hai. Aap sabhi ki upasthiti ki wajah se aaj Mysore ki is pavitra bhoomi par mujhe ek alag hi oorja anubhav ho rahi hai. Main aap sabhi santon ke charanon me saadar pranam karta hoon.

Sathiyo, Mysore Bharat ki anaadi sanskritik cheetna ka kendra raha hai. Yahan ke mandir, yahan ki paramparayein aur utsav, Mysore ka Dussehra, yahan ke bhavya mahal, iska sangeet aur sahitya, Bharat ki virasat is shahar ke kan-kan me rachi-basi hai. Aur yeh dekhkar bahut santosh milta hai ki shahar ne is virasat ko usi kushalata se sehja bhi hai, sanwara bhi hai. Shri Ramakrishna Ashram aur Swami Vivekananda ji ki smritiyan bhi Mysore ki virasat ka hi ek samriddh hissa hain. Kuch der pehle main us aitihasik bhavan me bhi gaya tha jahan Swami Vivekananda ji ne apne pravas ke dauran kuch samay bitaya tha. Karnataka ke kitne hi vidwan aur manishi is sthan se jude rahe. Rashtrakavi Kuvempu jaise mahapurush jab Mysore me the, unka bhi Ramakrishna Ashram se gehra judaav tha. Aaj Swami Vivekananda Cultural Youth Centre yaani 'Viveka Smaraka' ke udghatan ke zariye hum is mission ko naya vistaar de rahe hain. Iske liye main Shri Ramakrishna Ashram aur isse jude sabhi vidwanon ko hardik badhai deta hoon.

Sathiyo, vyakti se vibhooti ka nirmaan ekayek nahi hota. Iske liye hamein jeevan ki anaginat pareekshaayein deni hoti hain, sadhana karni hoti hai, tap karna hota hai, khud ko tapana padta hai, khapana padta hai. Khyati ke baad toh sansar peechhe khada hota hai, lekin tap ke samay mahaan vibhootiyon ko pehchanna aasaan nahi hota. Jo sadhak ko tapasyain me pehchanta hai, woh uski siddhi ka sahabhaagi bhi hota hai. Isliye bhaiyon-behnon, Swami Vivekananda ji ke jeevan me Mysore ka itna aham yogdan tha. Kareeb 130 varsh pehle Swami ji ek yuva sanyasi ke roop me bhraman karte hue Mysore aaye the. Tab unki prasiddhi vaisi nahi thi, lekin vichar utne hi oonche the. Us samay unhe jin logon ne pehchana, jo unke saath jude, unme se ek pramukh naam Mysore ke tatkaalin Maharaja ka bhi tha. Jab Swami Vivekananda ji America me the, toh unhone wahan se Mysore ke tatkaalin Maharaja ko ek patra likha tha. Usme unhone swayam Maharaja ke sahyog ka ullekh kiya tha.

Sathiyo, Swami Vivekananda ji ne Bharatiya cheetna ko aatmasaat kiya tha. Unhone shastron ko aatmasaat kiya tha, jo kehte hain: *Param paropkaraarth yo jeevati sa jeevati*—arthat vaastav me wahi jee-ta hai jo doosron ke liye jee-ta hai. Isi mantra par chalte hue Mysore ke is Shri Ramakrishna Ashram ne pichhle varsh apne 100 saal poore kiye hain. Shiksha aur swasthya se judi seveayein hon ya prakritik aapda me rahat kaarya, Shri Ramakrishna Ashram ne Swami Vivekananda ji ki virasat ko jeevant rakha hai. Mujhe vishwas hai Viveka Smaraka sadhakon aur shraddhaluon ke liye aatmik utthan ka ek teerth banega. Swami Vivekananda ji ki prerna thi—'Shiv gyaane jeev seva', yaani jeev ki seva hi Shiv ki seva hai. Hamein is Viveka Smaraka me isliye aise yuvaon ko gadhna hai jinke liye samaaj aur rashtra ke hit, gareeb, vanchit ke dukh apne sukh-dukh se oopar hon, jin yuvaon ke liye 'Rashtra Pratham' hi sarvopari mantra ho, Maa Bharti ki seva hi jinka jeevan lakshya ho.

Sathiyo, Swami Vivekananda ji yuvaon ki shiksha ko rashtra nirmaan ka majboot aadhar maante the. Unhone swayam yeh dekha tha ki kaise duniya ke desh apne yuvaon ko shikshit bana rahe hain. Lekin durbhagya se tab Bharat gulaam tha, Bharat ke yuva bhi gulaami ki zanjeeron me jakde hue the. Us daur se lekar ab tak Bharat ke yuva ne ek lambi yatra tay ki hai. Bharat ka jo yuva kabhi zanjeeron me tha, aaj wo yuva duniya ke vikas ko gati de raha hai. Aaj Bharat ki arthavyavastha sabse tezi se aage badh rahi hai—kiski wajah se? Bharat ke yuvaon ke saamarthya se! Aaj Bharat me duniya ka teesra sabse bada startup ecosystem hai—kiski wajah se? Bharat ke yuvaon ke saamarthya se! Aaj Bharat duniya ka doosra sabse bada mobile phone nirmata ban chuka hai—kiski wajah se? Bharat ke yuvaon ke saamarthya se! Aaj Bharat se bane digital solutions duniya ke anek deshon ke liye prerna ban rahe hain—kiski wajah se? Bharat ke yuvaon ke saamarthya se! Aaj Bharat semiconductor manufacturing ki nayi yatra par tezi se aage badh raha hai—kiski wajah se? Bharat ke yuvaon ke saamarthya se! Aaj Bharat AI, DeepTech, Space Technology aur Innovation ke naye adhyay likh raha hai—kiski wajah se? Bharat ke yuvaon ke saamarthya se! Abhi kuch din pehle hi humne dekha hai, Bharat ke yuvaon ne antariksh me kamaal kar diya hai—pehli baar yuvaon ka banaya hua ek private company ka rocket antariksh tak pahuncha hai. Aaj koi bhi sector ho, Bharat ka yuva apna damkham dikha raha hai. Hum sabhi apne aas-paas dekh rahe hain, aaj chhote se chhote vyavasay me laga yuva Bharat ki digital economy ko aage badha raha hai, wo Mudra loan ke zariye nayi shuruaat kar raha hai, wo desh ki startup revolution ko lead kar raha hai. Aatmanirbhar Bharat ka abhiyaan ho, Make in India ka abhiyaan ho, iski safalta ke peechhe Bharat ke yuvaon ki hi taakat hai.

Sathiyo, kisi bhi desh me yuvaon ka saamarthya tabhi us rashtra ki shakti banta hai jab usse sahi avsar mile, jab usse sahi disha mile, aur jab usse apni hi dharti par bade sapne dekne, unhe poora karne ka bharosa mile. Aur isme bahut badi bhoomika hamare shiksha sansthanon ki hoti hai. Mujhe khushi hai ki aaj Bharat me shikshan sansthanon ki sankhya lagataar badh rahi hai. Pichhle 12 varshon me 650 se zyada nayi universities bani hain, pichhle 12 varshon me kareeb 14,000 naye colleges bane hain, 4,000 se zyada nayi ITIs banai gayi hain, 9 naye IIMs, 7 nayi IITs aur 13 naye AIIMS sthapit kiye gaye hain. Aaj desh me MBBS ki सीटें kareeb 1 lakh 4,000 ho chuki hain, medical colleges ki sankhya badhkar 823 ho gayi hai.

Sathiyo, ek aur hum education infrastructure ko modernize kar rahe hain, saath hi shiksha vyavastha ko bhi aadhunik zarooraton ke hisaab se dhaala ja raha hai. National Education Policy Bharat ke yuvaon ko 21st century ke anuroop taiyaar karne ka madhyam ban rahi hai. Sathiyo, skooli shiksha me bhi bade star par kaam hua hai. Desh bhar me 14,000 se zyada PM SHRI schools viksit kiye gaye hain. Shiksha keval kitaabi na rahe, bachhon me innovation aur scientific temperament viksit ho, iske liye 10,000 se zyada Atal Tinkering Labs sthapit hui hain.

Sathiyo, Swami Vivekananda ji rashtra utthan ke liye shiksha ke saath-saath samanta ko bhi utna hi aham maante the. Shiksha me bhedbhav na ho, avsaron me bhedbhav na ho—aaj ye desh ka vision ban gaya hai. Aaj betiyan bhi Sainik Schools me admission le rahi hain, NDA me betiyan rashtra ke liye yogdan de rahi hain. Aaj medical aur engineering jaisi padhai maatribhasha me karne ki suvidha ho gayi hai. Isse bhasha ke aadhar par avsaron me hone wale bhedbhav par lagaam lag rahi hai.

Sathiyo, yuva shakti ka swasth, anushasit aur aatmavishwas se bhare rehna utna hi zaroori hai. Jo desh khelon ko mahatva dete hain, wahan ke yuvaon me in teeno gunon ka tezi se vikas hota hai. Isliye aaj khelon ko rashtra nirmaan ki ek mahatvapurna dhara ke roop me dekha ja raha hai. Khelo India Youth Games, University Games, Para Games aur Winter Games jaise aayojanon ne desh bhar ke yuvaon ko apni pratibha dikhane ka manch diya hai. Aaj desh bhar me aadhunik sports infrastructure ka vistaar ho raha hai. Khelo India Centers ke madhyam se suvidhaayein badh rahi hain, gaonon se lekar chhote shaharon tak nayi pratibhaon ki pehchan ki ja rahi hai. Aur sathiyo, aaj is abhiyaan ke parinaam bhi saaf-saaf dikhai de rahe hain. Antarrashtriya khel pratiyogitaon me Bharat ka pradarshan behtar hota ja raha hai, hamare khiladi naye-naye keertimaan bana rahe hain, hamari betiyan desh ka gaurav badha rahi hain.

Sathiyo, Swami Vivekananda ji ki tarah hamein Viveka Smaraka bade uddeshya ke liye kaarya karne ki prerna deta hai. Aise sthan par main hamare yuvaon se aagrah karna chahta hoon. Sathiyo, Mysore ko aksar Bharat ke sabse swachh shaharon me sthan mila hai, main iske liye yahan ke logon ko hriday se badhai deta hoon. Kya is avsar par hum yeh sankalp le sakte hain ki bhavishya me swachhata ke sankalp ko nirantar majboot karte rahenge? Hamein yahan ki prakritik sampadaon ko sanrakshit karna chahiye. Hamein paryavaran se jude sankalp lene chahiye. Hamein paani ki har boond bachane ke liye mission mode me kaam karna hoga.

Sathiyo, aaj hi yahan Valmiki Ramayana ke Kannada anuvaad ka vimochana bhi hua hai. Main Kannada bhasha se judi ek appeal bhi karna chahta hoon. Karnataka mahaan lekhak aur kaviyon ki dharti hai. Kannada bhasha, Kannada bhaashi log hamesha se padhne ki sanskriti ke liye jaane jaate hain. Smartphone ke is yug me is swabhaav ko aur badhava milna chahiye. Kya hum yuva peedhi ko Kannada sahitya se gehrai se judne ke liye prerit kar sakte hain?

Sathiyo, Mysore apne silk, chandan aur hastsilp ke liye prasiddh hai. Jab bhi hum kisi ko uphaar dein, toh kya hum yeh sunishchit kar sakte hain ki woh kisi Bharatiya ke hatho aur paseene se bani cheez ho? Isse hamari sanskriti ko badhava milega, saath hi ek aur parivaar ko aarthik sahayata bhi milegi.

Sathiyo, Swami Vivekananda ji ne jis tarah ke Bharat ka sapna dekha tha, usse poora karne ka daayitva aaj ki peedhi par hai. Mujhe vishwas hai Viveka Smaraka iske liye ek oorja srot ka kaam karega. Main ek baar phir yahan ekatra poojya sant janon, guru-bandhuon aur Mysore ke logon ko naman karte hue meri vaani ko viram deta hoon.

Aap sabka bahut-bahut dhanyavaad!
